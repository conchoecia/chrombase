# Schultz et al. 2026 citation-validation snapshot

This directory is a copyright-minimized evidence snapshot for the genome
assemblies analyzed in Schultz et al. (2026), “Topological mixing and
irreversibility in animal chromosome evolution.”

The tables contain bibliographic and assembly facts, original adjudications,
and short evidence fragments needed to verify those adjudications. Quoted
fragments are deduplicated and linked to a DOI/PMID, source document, section or
page locator, and source-block hash. They do not substitute for the papers.

Third-party excerpts remain the property of their respective rights holders.
They are not licensed under this repository's GPL-3.0 license. Where an article
has an explicit open license, its URL is recorded. Otherwise a fragment is
included only as a short quotation for scholarly verification and commentary.

The snapshot deliberately excludes complete papers, abstracts, figures, tables,
supplements, email addresses, correspondence, credentials, and local paths.

See [`docs/citation_validation.md`](../../docs/citation_validation.md) for the
evidence hierarchy and rebuild commands.
